# 5S Warehouse Workplace Audit — Simulated Report

## Purpose
Assess three fictional work areas using the 5S framework and turn observations into trackable corrective actions.

## Scoring
Each S is scored from 0 to 5:
- 0: Not implemented
- 1: Very poor
- 2: Needs improvement
- 3: Acceptable
- 4: Good
- 5: Excellent

Maximum score is 25 per area. Completion percentage = total score / 25 × 100.

## Findings to discuss
The sample audit highlights opportunities around storage labels, cleaning routines, visual standards and recurring audits. These are invented observations for demonstration, not findings from a real facility.

## Improvement cycle
1. Assign an owner to every action.
2. Set a realistic due date.
3. Re-audit the same area after actions are completed.
4. Compare scores and attach evidence.
5. Keep unresolved safety concerns within the site's formal safety reporting process.

## Limitation
A checklist score is a screening tool, not proof that a workplace is safe or compliant. Serious hazards require immediate escalation through the applicable site procedure.
